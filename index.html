    (06)Method

    How the numbers were put together.

    How this was put together
    • Announcements are taken as-is from the event sheets (Davos 2024, Davos 2025, US/Korea 2024, Japan 2025, Global Summit Dec 2025). Official totals are the government's figures; the Global Summit sheet lists 41 of the 75 MoUs.
    • TG-iPASS: the homepage no longer links the year-wise company lists, but the drill-down page (ipass.telangana.gov.in/TSHOMEDRILLDOWN.aspx?STATUS=A&FINANCIALYEAR=2024-2025, change the year) still returns them. All 8,135 units for FY23-24 to FY26-27 were downloaded on 16 Nov 2026.
    • Matching: each company name was searched in all unit names. Only units of the same company count as found. No news or other web sources were used for the match.
    • Timing: TG-iPASS gives only the financial year (Apr–Mar). An approval in a year that ended before the event is shown as "pre-event", since it cannot be a result of it.
    • "Found" means approved in TG-iPASS; the portal does not show whether production has started. Power and renewable projects and IT/GCC offices are mostly cleared outside TG-iPASS, so "not found" is decisive for factories but not for those.
