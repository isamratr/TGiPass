How to read the numbers.

About this data
  • "Investment announced" is the government's headline figure for each event (Davos 2024, US/Korea 2024, Davos 2025, Japan 2025, Global Summit 2025).
  • "Investment came / invested" counts only TG-iPASS approvals for the same company (the Invested column on each event sheet).
  • Company-wise announced amounts add up to less than the headline totals, because many announcements did not state an amount ("Not stated").
  • Group companies doing something else, and companies with a similar name, are listed under "What was delivered" but not counted.
  • TG-iPASS records approvals, not whether production has started. Power, renewable and IT/GCC projects are mostly cleared outside TG-iPASS.
